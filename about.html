OUR PRACTICE

Dentistry built around the patient in front of us.

Urban Dental Solutions is a general dentistry practice based at Nkulumane 12 Medical Centre in Bulawayo. The practice offers a full range of everyday dental care — routine cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, tooth replacement and whitening — inside a modern treatment room designed to put patients at ease.

As a small, focused practice, appointments run Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm, with time set aside to explain each treatment before it starts. The aim is straightforward: keep dentistry simple, comfortable and close to home.

Whether you're in for a six-month check-up or need treatment for a specific problem, the approach stays the same — clear communication, a clean and modern setting, and care that doesn't feel rushed.

Every instrument sterilised between patients.
